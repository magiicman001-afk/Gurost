Good to know

Questions, answered

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month to month and you can cancel any time from your account or at the front desk.

I have never trained before. Is that okay?

Yes, most of our members started as beginners. Your free first session is with a coach who will show you around and match you to the right classes.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Pause for up to two months a year, for holidays, injury or anything else, at no charge.

What do I need to bring?

Trainers, water and a towel. Mats, bars, bikes and kettlebells are all here.
