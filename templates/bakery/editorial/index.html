02The method

Three things we never rush

i.

The dough

Mixed by hand and left overnight to build flavour. Nothing is hurried.

ii.

The butter

Real butter, folded into every croissant, twenty-seven layers at a time.

iii.

The bake

Loaves go onto a hot stone before sunrise, so they are warm when you arrive.

{{business_name}} began with a single oven and a jar of starter, and a simple idea: that bread should taste of something. Every morning we mix, shape and bake in small batches, using British flour from mills we know by name.

We mill nothing ourselves, but we care where it comes from. The butter is the real thing, the eggs come from a farm down the road, and the coffee from a roaster who pays growers fairly.

What is not sold by closing goes to people who can use it. It is a small bakery with a long way of doing things, and we would not change a minute of it.

Good bread takes time. We simply give it plenty.{{owner_name}}, head baker
