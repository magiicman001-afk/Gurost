How we bake

Three simple steps

Mix & rest

Flour, water, salt and starter are mixed by hand, then left alone to build flavour overnight.

Shape & prove

Each loaf is shaped one at a time and given the time it needs, never a minute less.

Bake at dawn

Loaves go onto the hot stone before sunrise, so they are warm when you walk in.
