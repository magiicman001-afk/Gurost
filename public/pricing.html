Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later? expand_more

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will be pro-rated for the remainder of your billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept? expand_more

We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe, our secure payment processor. Custom plans can be billed by invoice.

Is there a free trial for paid plans? expand_more

There's no separate trial period, but our Free plan gives you 3 real builds a month at no cost, so you can try the actual product before ever paying.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits? expand_more

Yes, we provide special pricing for eligible non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Please contact our support team.
