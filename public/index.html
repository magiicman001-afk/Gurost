Common Inquiries

Do I need any technical knowledge to start? expand_more

Absolutely not. Gurost is designed for zero-barrier entry. If you can talk about your business, you can build a site. Our AI handles all the code, hosting, and design architecture.

Can I connect my own custom domain? expand_more

Yes, starting from the Pro plan, you can connect any custom domain you own. We also provide SSL certificates automatically to ensure your site is secure.

How does the AI Business Assistant work? expand_more

The Business Assistant is a custom-trained AI bot specifically for your site. It can handle customer inquiries, book appointments, and provide product information 24/7 based on your site's content.

Is Gurost SEO-friendly? expand_more

Gurost generates sites with clean, semantic HTML and lightning-fast loading speeds. The AI also optimizes meta tags, headings, and image alt-text automatically to ensure the best search engine visibility.
